WHAT DID WE ACTUALLY COLLECT?

Tracked, in this tab only: which objects you hovered, clicked and revisited; their order and how long each stayed open; which statement you chose, how long it took and whether you changed it; how many times you clicked in the particle field; sampled movement counts (a tally, not coordinates); how long you stayed still; whether you reached the edges or centre of the screen; hidden fragments found; time spent in each scene.

How observations were made: each sentence on the report is an if-statement over those numbers. If the number is zero, the sentence does not appear.

Classification: REVISITOR if you returned to an object or reopened your choice. Otherwise EXPLORER if you opened 4 or more objects. Otherwise OBSERVER. It is a game, not a personality test.

Not collected: your name, photos, location, cursor coordinates, device details or anything typed. Nothing was sent anywhere, no cookies or storage were used, and it all disappears when this tab closes.
